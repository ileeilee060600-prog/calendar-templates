Calendar Templates

Press Alt+T on Google Calendar to plan your day with templates.

Connect Google Calendar

Lets the planner see your day and add blocks with their color and reminders. Your calendar data goes only between your browser and Google; there is no other server. Without connecting, templates open Google’s own event editor, prefilled.

Advanced: use your own Google Cloud client
  1. In the Google Cloud console, create a project and enable the Google Calendar API.
  2. Set up the Google Auth Platform as External and add yourself as a test user.
