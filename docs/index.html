Calendar Templates

Event templates and a fast day planner for Google Calendar.

Add to Chrome

Templates

Save the blocks you schedule again and again, with their title, length, color, reminders, calendar and notes. Reminders default to “at time of event”.

One keystroke

Press Alt+T on Google Calendar, pick a template and press Enter. It lands in the next free slot of the day, or at the time you type: “deep work 14:30 45m”.

Plan visually

A live timeline of the day: drag templates onto it, move and resize your blocks, draw a range, or click a free window to fill it.

Private

Your calendar data goes only between your browser and Google. No servers of ours, no analytics, no ads.

How it works

  1. Install the extension and press Connect to allow it to see and add events in Google Calendar.
  2. Open Google Calendar and press Alt+T.
  3. Choose or drag templates to build your day. Every change has Undo.
